Suite v3 results — the new number axis

This page introduces the assistant benchmark's numbers on the new suite. The three tables in §1 put the same assistants on three axes so the change can be read left to right: the original v2.2 suite under the old 480-tick "human pace" (the 2026-08 sweep), the same suite under the tight fire timers, and suite v3 (2/2/3/4/4 forced errors per episode with at least half of them dish-ruining, content-hard questions, proactive alerts) under the tight timers. All rows are the average-person cook (questionnaire 2·2·2); the suite-v3 sweep runs in the frames setting only (the assistant sees the rendered kitchen, never a text state map), and the SILENT floor — which sees nothing either way — comes from the text-mode run of the same root. Per-persona tables follow once the sweep has filled in. The metric set of 2026-08-27 — success, efficiency, detection (timing and quality) — is entirely engine-graded: in-time success = 1 only if the engine verdict is won and the episode ended within 2 × (reference + allowance) ticks (no partial credit; a rejected serve is 0 whatever is on the plate); TT = 0 if timed out, else linear from 1 at the reference time to 0 at the cap; CC = 1 − speech time over the cook's actions ÷ episode ticks; efficiency = (a·TT + c·CC)/(a + c) for dishes won in time; DT = prevented 1 / correct flag before commit 0.5 / else 0; P, R, F1 over forced errors with a late flag counted as a miss; QA accuracy = judge-graded answers with the per-family rubric (correct 1, partially correct 0.5); proactive score = 1 at the moment the item is done, falling to 0 at the end of its ready window, 0 if spoken early, late or never. The fourth score, assistance quality v4 (the per-utterance rubric graded by a third-party model), is built separately and will appear on this page when its scorer runs; the old judge SQ, the old assistance-quality composite and the cook's exit survey are cancelled. Rows fill in as the pilot runs; a full sweep replaces them once authorised.

1c · Assistance quality v4 (suite v3, frames; the one third-party-graded score)

The per-round rubric (ten categories, fifteen items): every assistant round is graded by a judge from another model family (Gemini 3.7 Flash for GPT and local assistants, GPT-5.4 mini for Gemini and local; local assistants by both, averaged — flash-tier judges by ruling), with the engine's context stamp, the frame the assistant saw, the previous rounds and the cook's reaction as evidence. A round scores the weighted fraction of applicable items met, 0 if a must-item fails; the episode is the mean over rounds. Per-category columns are the share of applicable items passed in that category. must-fail = share of rounds zeroed by a must-item (a false claim, an invented thing, an unexecutable action, an ignored intent or stop request, unsafe advice). Episodes graded so far are shown; the column fills as the grader runs.

Early = spoke before it was done; late = after it burnt or was taken off; no window = the item never reached done after the request (the episode ended first).
