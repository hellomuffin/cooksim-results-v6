Suite v3 results — the new number axis

This page introduces the assistant benchmark's numbers on the new suite. The three tables in §1 put the same assistants on three axes so the change can be read left to right: the original v2.2 suite under the old 480-tick "human pace" (the 2026-08 sweep), the same suite under the tight fire timers, and suite v3 (2/2/3/4/4 forced errors per episode with at least half of them dish-ruining, content-hard questions, proactive alerts) under the tight timers. All rows are the average-person cook (questionnaire 2·2·2) in the text-state-map observation setting. Metrics are evaluation v3 with the 2026-08-27 rulings: in-time success = 1 only if the dish was won within 2 × (reference + allowance) ticks; DT = prevented 1 / correct flag before commit 0.5 / else 0; P, R, F1 over forced errors with a late flag counted as a miss; assistance quality v3 = (b·(DT+F1) + 2·SQ)/(2b+2); QA accuracy = judge-graded answers (correct 1, partially correct 0.5); proactive score = 1 at the moment the item is done, falling to 0 at the end of its ready window, 0 if spoken early, late or never. Rows fill in as the pilot runs; a full sweep replaces them once authorised.

3 · Proactive alerts (Q10, suite v3)

The cook asks once — “give me a shout the moment the X is done” — and the assistant must speak inside the item's ready window [done, burnt) without being asked again. Early = spoke before it was done; late = after it burnt or was taken off; no window = the item never reached done after the request (the episode ended first).
