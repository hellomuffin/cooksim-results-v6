What changed
  • The layout is reversed: world engine → simulated user → visual assistant. Arrow directions retain their semantics; only the user acts on the world.
  • The persona card is 14% narrower and the scheduled-event card 23% narrower than the preceding revision. Their combined footprint is centered on the simulated user, rather than stretched across the remaining canvas.
  • The user has the strongest filled header and a larger title. Engine and assistant headers are lighter; the assistant is a compact supporting element. All three block bottoms remain aligned.
  • Actor headings have more clearance above their dividers. The world–user gap is wider, and interface labels have more vertical separation from their arrows. The existing editing URL is unchanged.
  • Persona examples read “Expert in skill,” “Low AI trust,” and “…”. A wider event card shows an execution error, a question, and a recipe change as short phrases.
  • Your labels from commit 7281ecb are retained: “render,” “message,” “observation,” and “state change.” “Action control” remains removed. The prior edited draw.io file is untouched.
Academic interpretation and production checks
