What changed
  • The engine now has four aligned rows: Task, render, World state, and Action space. Larger paired views replace thumbnail-sized observations.
  • The graph identifies objects and relations: Patty → in → Pan; Cook → faces → Pan; Cook → holds → Plate. Secondary fields show cooked state, active heat, and plate contents count. The cook node is distinguished from object nodes.
  • Persona examples read “Expert in skill,” “Low AI trust,” and “…”. A wider event card shows an execution error, a question, and a recipe change as short phrases.
  • Your labels from commit 7281ecb are retained: “render,” “message,” “observation,” and “state change.” “Action control” remains removed. The prior edited draw.io file is untouched.
Academic interpretation and production checks
  • The camera images are authentic paired views from the same CookSim tick. The graph is an illustrative partial state, not a reconstruction of that frame. Its entity attributes and relations correspond to station contents, ingredient preparation, cook orientation, and held objects in the simulator.
  • Event phrases are drawn from the cheeseburger case b3_medium_nops_hard_map_3: leaving the pan, asking the next step, and adding onion. The last phrase intentionally does not specify preparation, because an older case definition had inconsistent wording about chopped versus raw onion.
  • Three local render passes addressed image scale, graph label spacing, action-chip fit, and node/attribute hierarchy. Final checks cover font loading, text overlap, page bounds, actual PDF size, and native editing in diagrams.net.
  • Visual comparisons used τ-Voice, LATTE, Collaborative Gym, and SensiBLE from the reference board. These comparisons guide the design; they are not evidence of an objective aesthetic ranking.
