Design and source notes
  • Three text roles: sans-serif system labels, a serif-italic task instruction, and monospaced action examples.
  • The user’s internal planning/control cycle is distinct from communication and environment feedback.
  • Both observations come from the same tick in the existing cheeseburger episode, not generated imagery. Action phrases are examples, not a claimed transcript of that tick.
  • Recipe and command semantics checked against CookSim’s current recipes.py and agents/highlevel.py.
  • Visual references inspected: LATTE and m&m’s, especially separation of natural-language content and executable actions.
  • Previous proposals and editing files remain unchanged: A–D archive.
