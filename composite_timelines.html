The gray blocks are the plan's subgoals; the green diamonds mark where the plate actually changes (the engine's anchor currency); the numbered pins are the four injections in firing order. Everything drawn is recomputed from the engine — plan structure, plate milestones, deviated command blocks, and each question's ground truth at its anchor.

Pins: square = injected error · circle = question · diamond = plate milestone. Hover any pin, block or diamond for details; the table under each card spells out anchors, deviated blocks and ground truths.

Every one of the 50 episodes below was written by hand, one at a time, from a replay-verified inventory of every realizable error instance (378 usable) and of every question kind's ground truth at every plate anchor. Across the suite no error instance is used twice (100 distinct (cell, error, parameters) triples for 100 error slots) and no question is asked twice (100 distinct questions); inside an episode the two errors carry different labels and sit at different moments of the plan, and the two questions are of different kinds and are anchored where their answer is genuinely discriminating (a station that is still cooking vs done, a board that still holds a chopped item, a step done long ago, a place the plan never visits, a long ordered list). Each card ends with the designer's rationale for that episode.

To verify an episode: read its pins left to right and ask (1) does each error make sense at that plate stage, (2) is each question answerable — and natural — right where it fires, (3) is the order something a real kitchen could produce?
