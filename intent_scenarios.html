Why there is no hard take of plan_change or side_quest. Under the hard paradigm the engine splices the cook's announced sequence into its queue with no subgoal id, and the episode crashes as soon as the first of those commands finishes — before any report is written. Filed as ECR-7 for the engine owner; it is not a property of the scenario. qa_probe asks a question rather than queueing a sequence, so it is unaffected and carries the hard/soft comparison on this page.

Whether the intent was carried out is read from the plate and the stations, not from the harness's released_reason — that label reports cook_talked_out_of_it on an episode where the cook plated exactly what it had announced, so taking it at face value would have put the opposite of the truth on this page. Where the two disagree the card says so.
