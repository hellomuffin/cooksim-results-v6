Counting rules, stated once and applied everywhere on this page: a detection is a flag whose label is this case's error, at any tick; in-window is the stricter valid verdict, graded against the live event-anchored window the report publishes. A true_observation is neutral — a flag the kitchen confirms is never charged as a false alarm, even when its label is not this case's error. False-alarm counts are recomputed here from the flag verdicts and the report's stored list is checked against them.
