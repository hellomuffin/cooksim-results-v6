Open-weight VLMs along the episode

Interaction quality of every assistant line, placed where it fell in its episode. x = progress through the episode: the tick of the line over the episode's last tick, in ten bins of 10%. y = share of the lines in that bin that were clean in the rubric category (no applicable item failed): factual grounding, situational relevance, actionable guidance. Six polled open-weight VLM seats of run 1, CookSim rerun v6; 150 episodes per seat, pooled over the three cook personas. Each panel's heading carries Δ = last third − first third of the episode.

Late minus early, per seat

Mean over lines in the last third of the episode minus the mean over lines in the first third. Negative values are worse late.

Reading

Factual grounding falls for every seat from the first third to the last third of the episode (−0.13 to −0.23, MiniCPM-o the steepest); actionable guidance falls for every seat (Muse Glimmer −0.18, Qwen3.8-27B −0.15, Molmo2-8B −0.09, InternVideo3-8B −0.08); situational relevance falls for every seat but less (−0.04 to −0.11).

Caveat: progress is measured in game ticks, so late bins over-represent long episodes (timeouts, burns), which are the harder situations. A within-episode comparison would separate "later in the same episode" from "episodes that run long".
