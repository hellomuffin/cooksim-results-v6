Task, command, frame, and validation manifest

Why this particular error and these two users?

The user is about to add lettuce to a completed cheeseburger whose recipe contains no lettuce. The addition is visually unmistakable, unlike a repeated cheese portion. The engine has no operation for removing only the unwanted ingredient, so recovery empties the food at the bin and retains the plate. We executed early prevention, invalid serving, immediate recovery, and a pause-to-question followed by recovery to verify the depicted consequences.

The high-trust user accepts the correction and asks for the next step. The low-trust user questions the assistant’s recipe claim, receives checkable ingredient information, then resumes independently and requests fewer prompts. These choices follow the implemented persona dispositions. Both can recover: the illustration concerns interaction differences, not a claim that one persona necessarily succeeds and the other fails.
