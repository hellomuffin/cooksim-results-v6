# Centered system overview with horizontal world rows

Revision of the vertical overview variant, preserving all wording and every
conversation cell. The world engine uses three horizontal rows: single-line
task description, paired observations, and a single row of semantic actions.

The assistant is on top, the human in the middle, and the world engine below.
The actor cards share a centerline; the engine retains the author's placement.
Persona and scheduled-event cards are
stacked to the human's right and extend beyond the engine's right edge.
Arrows preserve the interfaces: human actions go to the world, world feedback
returns to the human, and communication is between human and assistant only.
The human and assistant cards now reuse the exact native groups underlying
the current manuscript Figure 1: the same dimensions, fonts, icons, and internal
spacing. Persona and scheduled-event cards restore the same reference heading
gaps, line pitch, and padding. The event card is slightly wider to retain right
padding around its longest phrase. The conversation panel is uniformly enlarged
to retain matching panel heights; no dialogue is rewritten or reflowed.

The author's horizontal world-engine and subcaption adjustments from commit
`9cbe597` are preserved. See `actor_match.json` for the source comparison.

All labels, shapes, icons, and arrows remain editable. The observation images
retain their original pixels. Prior variants and the Overleaf manuscript are
unchanged. Editing the native source does not automatically refresh exports.
