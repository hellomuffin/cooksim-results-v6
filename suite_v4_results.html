3 · Assistance quality v4 (the one third-party-graded score)

The per-round rubric (ten categories, fifteen items): every assistant round is graded by a judge from another model family (Gemini 3.7 Flash for GPT and local assistants, GPT-5.4 mini for Gemini and local; local assistants by both, averaged — flash-tier judges by ruling), with the engine's context stamp, the frame the assistant saw, the previous rounds and the cook's reaction as evidence. A round scores the weighted fraction of applicable items met, 0 if a must-item fails; the episode is the mean over rounds. Per-category columns are the share of applicable items passed in that category. must-fail = share of rounds zeroed by a must-item (a false claim, an invented thing, an unexecutable action, an ignored intent or stop request, unsafe advice). Episodes graded so far are shown; the column fills as the grader runs.

5 · Proactive alerts (Q10)

The cook asks once — “give me a shout the moment the X is done” — and the assistant must speak inside the item's physical ready window [done, done+grace+burn) without being asked again. The alert is the earliest line a judge classifies as asserting that this item is ready now: a promise to tell later, a denial that it is ready, and a readiness claim about another item are excluded. Early = asserted readiness before it was done; late = after the burn horizon; pre-empted = the cook took the food off before an alert was possible (unscored, not a miss); no window = the item never reached done after the request. Re-graded 2026-08-28 after the grading audit found the previous keyword rule scoring the assistant's acknowledgement of the request as a failed alert; the mean score over the same requests rose from 0.23 to 0.56 and the ordering changed.
