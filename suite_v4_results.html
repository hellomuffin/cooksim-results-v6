Suite v4 results — the new number axis

Sweep v4 (launched 2026-09-05 07:04 UTC, engine 4d15175f2d). Same suite v3 cases; what changed since the v3 sweep: the cook prompt is restructured (kitchen rules, no plan or subgoal list, WHAT YOU KNOW = goal + goal change + past actions), the assistant no longer sees the cook's plan, every recipe is plating-order strict, the injected-error SYSTEM TASK is split into belief and attention framings with an engine hint that tells the cook whether the assistant's flag is correct (two trust levels), the assistant's reply latency is charged in ticks (ceil(seconds/1.2)) while the cook keeps moving, and a leave_unattended error is only injected while something is on the heat. Numbers here are NOT comparable with the v3 page. The page rebuilds every 30 minutes while the sweep runs; cases were queued in a tier-stratified order, so a partial table is balanced across tiers and personas.

The assistant benchmark on suite v3: 50 episodes, 2/2/3/4/4 forced errors per episode with at least half of them dish-ruining, content-hard questions, proactive alerts, all under the tight fire timers. Nine assistants and the SILENT floor, three simulated cooks, frames setting only (the assistant sees the rendered kitchen, never a text state map). Every score below except assistance quality v4 is read from the engine trace. What each number means — its formula, and the instruments behind the qualitative ones — is on the metrics page.

3 · Assistance quality v4 (the one third-party-graded score)

A round scores the weighted fraction of applicable items met, 0 if a must-item fails; the episode is the mean over rounds. Per-category columns are the share of applicable items passed in that category. must-fail = share of rounds zeroed by a must-item (a false claim, an invented thing, an unexecutable action, an ignored intent or stop request, unsafe advice). 5 · Proactive alerts (Q10)

The cook asks once — “give me a shout the moment the X is done” — and the assistant must speak inside the item's physical ready window [done, done+grace+burn) without being asked again. The alert is the earliest line a judge classifies as asserting that this item is ready now: a promise to tell later, a denial that it is ready, and a readiness claim about another item are excluded. Early = asserted readiness before it was done; late = after the burn horizon; pre-empted = the cook took the food off before an alert was possible (unscored, not a miss); no window = the item never reached done after the request. Re-graded 2026-08-28 after the grading audit found the previous keyword rule scoring the assistant's acknowledgement of the request as a failed alert; the mean score over the same requests rose from 0.23 to 0.56 and the ordering changed.
