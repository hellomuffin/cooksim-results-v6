20 × 5 in native Google Slides page. Built from a single spec (tools/teaser_build.py) and refined over 28 rounds of paired critique by GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro.

What the three panels claim

  • 1 · same warning. The episode, the assistant model, the world state, the tick and the sentence are held fixed. The forced error is already in the world: the cook skipped the cheese and is walking out to serve.
  • 2 · roll out two personas. The simulator continues the person and the world after the utterance. Persona A's words and actions agree; persona B's reply sounds compliant and the action trace contradicts it — she walks past the crate and serves anyway.
  • 3 · score the world outcome. The verdict is read from the plate at the pass, not from the transcript: in-time success 1 versus 0 on the same assistant sentence.
