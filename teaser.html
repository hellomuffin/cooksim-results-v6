The episode

One case of the v3 suite — b3_easy_nops_hard_map_4, a Caprese (chopped tomato + raw cheese) — run twice with the same assistant model (gemini-3.7-flash) and the same injected error: the cook never picks up the cheese and walks to the serving pass with tomato only. The only thing that differs between the two runs is the simulated person.

What the three panels claim

  • Rollout. The simulator does not stop at the utterance: the person and the world keep moving, and the figure shows the frames that follow.
  • Persona. The same correction, in the same world state, at the same tick, produces a different reply and a different trajectory depending on who is cooking.
  • Outcome-based evaluation. The verdict is read off the final plate by the engine, not judged from the transcript: order accepted versus rejected, in-time success 1 versus 0.
