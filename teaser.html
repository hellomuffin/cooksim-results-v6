The controlled comparison

A and D share a cook and differ only in the assistant; B and C receive the identical warning and differ only in the person. That is the whole argument of the paper in one picture: the assistant's sentence is not the outcome — the rollout is.

What the metrics show

  • in-time success separates D from the rest — the only binary the transcript cannot fake.
  • detection timing DT separates A (prevention, 1.0) from B and C (caught after the commit, 0.5) from D (missed, 0).
  • ticks to serve is the efficiency term: all three successes are wins, but the person's disposition costs 32 and 49 ticks.
  • assistant turns is the conversation cost that the persona-weighted efficiency term charges for.
  • guidance quality AQ is the judged rubric score, which drops for C's step-by-step hand-holding and collapses for D.
